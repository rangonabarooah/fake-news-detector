How It Works

  1. Clean the text

    The input is lowercased and common English stop words such as "the" and "and" are removed.

  2. Vectorize

    TF-IDF converts the words into numbers. Words that appear in many articles get less weight, and words that stand out get more.

  3. Classify

    A Passive Aggressive classifier looks at those numbers and decides which side of the line the article falls on: REAL or FAKE.

  4. Report

    The page shows the label, and the score shows how far the article sits from the decision line.
