How It Works

  1. Clean the text

    The input is lowercased and common English stop words such as "the" and "and" are removed.

  2. Vectorize

    TF-IDF converts the words into numbers. Words that appear in many articles get less weight, and words that stand out get more.

  3. Classify

    A Passive Aggressive classifier looks at those numbers and decides which side of the line the article falls on: REAL or FAKE.

  4. Report

    The page shows the label, and the score shows how far the article sits from the decision line.

About This Project

FakeNewsAI is a student project built with Flask, scikit-learn, and plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It was trained on the Fake and Real News dataset, which contains labeled articles from news sites and from unreliable sources.

On a held-out test split, the model reached about 99.5% accuracy. That figure is measured on articles from the same source as the training data. Accuracy is likely lower on news from other outlets, other years, or other topics.

Known limits: the model cannot verify claims, it can be fooled by writing that imitates real news, and it has no knowledge of current events.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the model check whether a claim is true?

No. It only looks at how the text is written and compares that to patterns in its training data.

What input works best?

Full article text works better than a short headline, because there are more words to weigh.

Is my text stored?

The app processes each request and returns a result. It does not save submitted text.

Why does the page take a moment to load?

The free hosting tier sleeps when no one has visited for a while. The first load wakes it up.
